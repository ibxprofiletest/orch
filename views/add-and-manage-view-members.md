# Add and Manage View Members

To **manage members in a view**, you must have the Owner or Editor role in it, or be an Owner or Editor of your workspace. To manage members, go to the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") by clicking on the view on the [tasks-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/tasks-page "mention") and then on the Members tab in the view's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention").&#x20;

From there, you can:

* Add members to the view;
* Remove members from the view;
* Invite new members;
* Assign roles.

## Adding Members to a View

* Navigate to the [tasks-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/tasks-page "mention");
* Find the view you want to add members to;
* Open the view's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by left-clicking the view;
* Open the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") in the view's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention");
* Click the `+` button to the right of the Members section;
* In the popup, select the members you want to add to the view, and then click the `Add member` button.

## Removing Members from a View

* Go to the [tasks-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/tasks-page "mention");
* Find the view you want to remove members from;
* Open task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by left-clicking the view;
* Open the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") in the view's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention");
* Right-click on the member you want to remove;
* Select `Remove member` from the context menu that appears.

## Setting Roles for Members

* Go to the [tasks-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/tasks-page "mention");
* Find the view where you want to assign roles;
* Open view's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by left-clicking the view;
* Open the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") in the view's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention");
* Right-click on the member, or click on their current role name;
* Select the role you want to assign to the member.

Here you can **read more about roles** in Orchestra: [user-roles](https://docs.orch.so/workspace/user-roles "mention")
