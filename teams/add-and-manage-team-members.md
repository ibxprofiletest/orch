# Add and Manage Team Members

To **manage members in a team**, you must have the Owner or Editor role in it, or be an Owner or Editor of your workspace. To manage team members, navigate to the team [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") by clicking on the `Info` icon in the top-right corner of the [teams-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/teams-page "mention") and then on the members tab.&#x20;

From there, you can:

* Add members to the team;
* Remove members from the team;
* Invite new members;
* Set roles for members.

## Adding Members to a Team

* Go to the [teams-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/teams-page "mention");
* In the [#teams-block](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/teams-page#teams-block "mention"), click on the team to which you want to add members;
* Open the team's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by clicking the `Info` icon in the top-right corner of the screen;
* Open the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") by clicking on it;
* Click the `+` button to the right of the Members section;
* In the popup, select the members you want to add to the team, and click the `Add member` button.

## Removing Members from a Team

* Navigate to the [teams-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/teams-page "mention");
* In the [#teams-block](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/teams-page#teams-block "mention"), click on the team from which you want to remove a member;
* Open the team's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by clicking the `Info` icon in the top-right corner of the screen;
* Open the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") by clicking on it;
* Right-click on the member you want to remove;
* Click `Remove member` from the context menu that appears.

## Inviting Members

You can **invite new users directly to a team** using the **team invite link**. Invited users will also join the workspace as members.

* Go to the [teams-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/teams-page "mention");
* In the [#teams-block](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/teams-page#teams-block "mention"), click on the team to which you want to invite a member;
* Open the team's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by clicking the `Info` icon in the top-right corner of the screen;
* Click the `Copy link` button in the the Invite Link section;
* Send the copied link to the user you want to invite.

## Set Roles for Team Members

* Go to the [teams-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/teams-page "mention");
* In the [#teams-block](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/teams-page#teams-block "mention"), click on the team in which you want to set member roles;
* Open the team's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by clicking the `Info` icon in the top-right corner of the screen;
* Right-click on the member or click on their current role name;
* Select the role you want to assign to the member.

For **more information about roles** in Orchestra, read here: [user-roles](https://docs.orch.so/workspace/user-roles "mention")
