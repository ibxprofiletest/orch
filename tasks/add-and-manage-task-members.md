# Add and Manage Task Members

To **manage members in a task**, you must have the Owner or Editor role in the task's project, or be an Owner or Editor of your workspace. To manage members, go to the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") by clicking on the task on the [tasks-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/tasks-page "mention") and then on the Members tab in the task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention").&#x20;

From there, you can:

* Add members to the task;
* Remove members from the task;
* Invite new members;
* Set roles for members.

## Add Members to a Task

* Navigate to the [tasks-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/tasks-page "mention");
* Open task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by clicking on the task you want to add members to;
* Open the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") in the task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention");
* Click the `+` button to the right of the Members section;
* In the popup, select the members you want to add to the task, and then click the `Add member` button.

## Remove Members from a Task

* Go to the [tasks-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/tasks-page "mention");
* Open task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by clicking on the task you want to remove members from;
* Open the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") in the task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention");
* Right-click on the member you want to remove;
* Select `Remove member` from the context menu that appears.

## Invite Members

You can **invite new users directly to a task** using the **task invite link**. Invited users will automatically  join the task's chat, as well as the task's project and workspace.

* Navigate to the [tasks-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/tasks-page "mention");
* Open task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by clicking on the task you want to invite a member to;
* Open the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") in the task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention");
* Click the `Copy link` button in the the Invite Link section;
* Send the copied link to the user you want to invite.

## Set Roles for Members

* Go to the [tasks-page](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/tasks-page "mention");
* Open task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention") by clicking on the task in which you want to set member roles;
* Open the [#members-tab](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar#members-tab "mention") in the task's [info-sidebar](https://docs.orch.so/pages-and-ui-elements/info-sidebar "mention");
* Right-click on the member, or click on their current role name;
* Select the role you want to assign to the member.

Here you can **read more about roles** in Orchestra: [user-roles](https://docs.orch.so/workspace/user-roles "mention")
